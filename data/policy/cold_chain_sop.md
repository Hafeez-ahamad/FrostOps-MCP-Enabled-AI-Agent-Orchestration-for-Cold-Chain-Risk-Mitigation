# Reefer Fleet Incident Procedure — Southern California Network
## Operating procedure for temperature, congestion and risk events
**Revision:** 1.0 | **Scope:** simulated fleet, illustrative procedure (not a real company document)

### 1. Cargo Temperature Breach
Every refrigerated trailer reports its cargo temperature continuously, and dispatch watches it against the cargo band.
* **Fresh band:** fresh perishables are carried between 0.0 °C and 4.0 °C.
* **Breach rule:** a reading above **4.0 °C** opens a cold-chain breach for that shipment straight away.
* **Response:** dispatch asks the driver to restart or reset the reefer unit first. When the ETA delay is more than **60 minutes**, the shipment also becomes a candidate for diversion to the nearest emergency cold-storage facility.

### 2. Port Congestion
Congestion at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach is the main cause of missed delivery windows.
* **Congestion rule:** when the port congestion level rises above **7.0** on the 0–10 scale, normal port routing is paused for port-bound shipments.
* **Response:** freight is not left waiting at the port. Active port-bound shipments are diverted to the Inland Empire Overflow Depot (San Bernardino) for cross-docking.

### 3. High-Risk Shipments
* **Escalation rule:** a shipment classed as **High Risk** whose delay probability is above **0.65** goes to the Tier 2 logistics manager for review.
